Hauntkit is a reusable foundation for characters who can do things, remember things, and live by the rules of their world.

01

Two real applications

Ghost Inn is a complete clue-driven mystery with four rooms and two endings. Lost Letters is a writing desk with tone changes, editing, undo, and a text export. Each owns its content and rules; both use the same session engine.

02

Choices that hold

Every action checks the current version before committing. Failed actions change nothing. Sessions are isolated. Saved worlds are validated before they're restored.

03

An honest little demo

The browser experiences use authored dialogue and letter templates. There is no hidden AI call or pretend model response. An AI can join through MCP and use exactly the same validated actions.

04

Your words stay yours

Browser saves live on your device. No account, analytics script, or letter delivery service is connected. Export a save to keep it, and clear the desk or reset the inn whenever you like.

BRING YOUR OWN CHARACTER

One world. Human or AI.

The open-source project includes a local stdio MCP server. Connect it to Kiro or another compatible client to start a session, observe the world, and perform validated actions. The server runs on your machine; this website does not expose a public command server.
